## AI Tools and Responsible Use

ASICODER tools are designed to assist with practical digital workflows.

Users should understand that:

- AI-enhanced images may not reproduce every original detail accurately.
- Image compression can reduce quality when aggressive settings are used.
- Background removal may need additional edge refinement.
- Plagiarism and grammar checkers may produce incomplete or imperfect results.
- AI writing tools do not replace proofreading, proper attribution, or independent judgment.

Always verify results before professional, commercial, or academic use.
